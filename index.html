TelegramToYT Bot

🤖 About Our App

TelegramToYT Bot is a specialized automation tool that allows users to upload videos from Telegram channels and chats directly to YouTube using secure API integration.

Key Features:
  • Secure Telegram bot integration
  • Direct YouTube API connectivity
  • Automated video processing and upload
  • User-friendly command interface
  • Metadata preservation and customization

🔒 Data Usage & Privacy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and data security. Our application:

  • Video Content: We temporarily process videos to facilitate upload to YouTube. Videos are not stored on our servers after processing.
  • Telegram Data: We only access videos you explicitly choose to upload through our bot commands.
  • YouTube Integration: We use YouTube API to upload videos to your authorized YouTube channel with your explicit permission.
  • Authentication: All API connections use secure OAuth 2.0 protocols.
  • Data Retention: We do not permanently store your video content or personal data.
Purpose of Data Collection: We collect and process video data solely to enable the upload functionality from Telegram to YouTube as requested by users through bot commands.

🛡️ How It Works

Our bot operates with a simple and secure process:

  1. Authorization: Users authorize both Telegram bot access and YouTube channel permissions
  2. Video Selection: Users send video upload commands through our Telegram bot
  3. Processing: Videos are securely processed and formatted for YouTube upload
  4. Upload: Videos are uploaded to the user's YouTube channel with specified metadata
  5. Confirmation: Users receive confirmation of successful upload
