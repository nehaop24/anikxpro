Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how TelegramToYT Bot ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Telegram to YouTube video upload service accessible through https://anikxpro.vercel.app.

Important: Our service facilitates video uploads from Telegram to YouTube. We prioritize data security and minimal data retention.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the necessary information to provide our video upload service:

  • Telegram Data: Video files you choose to upload through our bot commands
  • YouTube Authentication: OAuth tokens to access your YouTube channel (with your explicit permission)
  • Video Metadata: Title, description, and other metadata you provide for YouTube uploads
  • Usage Logs: Basic service usage information for troubleshooting and improvement
  • Contact Information: Email address only if you contact us for support

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and upload videos from Telegram to your YouTube channel
  • To authenticate and authorize API access to Telegram and YouTube
  • To provide technical support and respond to your inquiries
  • To improve our service functionality and user experience

3. Data Processing & Storage

Video Processing: Videos are temporarily processed in memory during upload. We do not store video files on our servers after successful upload to YouTube.
  • Temporary Processing: Videos are held temporarily during the upload process only
  • No Permanent Storage: Video content is automatically deleted after processing
  • Secure Transmission: All data transfers use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • API Tokens: Stored securely and used only for authorized operations

4. Third-Party Services

Our service integrates with:

  • Telegram Bot API: To receive video upload commands and files
  • YouTube Data API v3: To upload videos to your YouTube channel
  • Google OAuth 2.0: For secure authentication with YouTube

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Telegram Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. User Control & Consent

  • Explicit Consent: You must explicitly authorize both Telegram bot access and YouTube upload permissions
  • Selective Upload: You choose which videos to upload through specific bot commands
  • Revoke Access: You can revoke API permissions at any time through your Google account settings
  • Data Deletion: Contact us to delete any stored authentication data

6. Data Security

  • All communications use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.2+)
  • API credentials are stored using secure encryption methods
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • No video content is permanently stored on our servers

7. Data Retention

  • Video Content: Immediately deleted after successful upload
  • Authentication Tokens: Retained until you revoke access or request deletion
  • Usage Logs: Retained for 30 days for troubleshooting purposes
  • Support Communications: Retained for 1 year for reference

8. Your Rights

  • Access information about your API authorizations
  • Request deletion of stored authentication data
  • Revoke bot and YouTube API permissions at any time
  • Receive information about any data processing incidents

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. International Data Transfers

Our service may process data across different regions. All transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and use appropriate safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date indicates the most recent revision.
